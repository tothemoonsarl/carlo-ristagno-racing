The Driver

Ever since I was little, I've been passionate about motor racing. I still remember the last lap between Hamilton and Verstappen at the 2021 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix. It still gives me chills, and I dreamed that one day I could be in their place too.

At 10, I started driving an F1 simulator on PlayStation at home. Then at 12, I started rental karting in Payerne with my dad. That's where it all really began: laps, lap times, battles and the drive to do better every time out.

Summer 2025 was a turning point. For the first time in my life, I got behind the wheel of an X30 kart at the Circuit de l'Enclos in Levier. The noise, the smell, the 105 km/h top speed… I knew I could never do without it again.

On 27 and 28 September 2025, I raced in the fifth and final round of the Vega Trofeo in the Junior category. Practice sessions, qualifying and a race with 22 drivers on track, the emotions, the pressure, the adrenaline… I had never felt anything so powerful. That was the day I knew this world was mine.

In December 2025, I joined Team Spirit for winter training in Valencia, Spain: 4 crazy days to get ready for the 2026 Vega Trofeo championship.
